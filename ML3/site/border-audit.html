How this builds on prior work

SAM-X established radiograph sorting across many anatomical categories. DeGrave and colleagues intervened on chest X-ray markers to test shortcuts. Here the experimental question is whether changing edge intensities with a same-joint donor from a different source shifts predictions more than applying the same operation with a donor from the original source.

The intervention can remove peripheral anatomy and introduce seams. The within-source control applies the same operation, and center pixels are identical in both conditions. This pilot estimates sensitivity to these synthetic frames. It cannot isolate hospital, acquisition equipment or implant effects. The provisional knee labels were excluded from this analysis.
