Research

A narrow question, tested before looking

Can conventional pooled evaluation of public orthopaedic radiograph datasets substantially overestimate anatomical generalisation because joint labels are confounded with source and acquisition characteristics?

Pre-specified primary hypothesis

H1. Pooled-minus-decoupled balanced accuracy exceeds 0.10 for the primary models (clustered paired bootstrap, Holm correction over five primary models).

Result: supported for 5 of 5; gaps 0.31–0.50; Holm-adjusted p = 0.0025. Further secondary hypotheses (H2–H5) and all deviations are recorded in the frozen analysis plan.

Contributions

  1. A quantified gap between pooled and source-decoupled evaluation across handcrafted baselines, three frozen backbones and three fine-tuned networks.
  2. Evidence that features with no anatomy (dimensions, file metadata) reproduce pooled performance and fall to chance when decoupled.
  3. Characterisation of leave-one-source-out failure for implant-only sources, with explicit causal caveats.
  4. A reliability analysis: unknown-joint detection, calibration and selective prediction degrade under source shift.

What this study does not claim

  • No new classifier and no clinical tool.
  • No claim that these models fail in hospitals — there is no hospital-level external validation.
  • No causal claim about implants; they are a plausible source-associated confound.
  • No claim of being the first to describe shortcut learning in medical imaging; related work includes Badgeley 2019, DeGrave 2021 and Hill 2024 (knee). Our search was targeted, not systematic.
Strongest, safest sentenceIn these public collections, pooled evaluation overstated anatomical generalisation by 0.31–0.50 balanced accuracy on the three-class endpoint; part of the pooled performance is attributable to image properties unrelated to anatomy.
Project scopeThe models classify Hip, Knee or Shoulder. This project evaluates how joint-recognition models are tested; disease prediction and implant-brand identification are outside the implemented task.
