About

About this project

OrthoTrace is an independent research website built to present a source-controlled evaluation of orthopaedic joint recognition. It was designed from scratch; it is not affiliated with, or derived from, any commercial product.

Authors & affiliations

To be completed before submission (see the author and declarations template in the project). Until then, no authorship, funding or conflict-of-interest statement is made on this site.

Status

  • Manuscript: draft, not peer-reviewed.
  • Clinician verification of the 94 FracAtlas knee labels: pending.
  • Ethics: public, de-identified datasets only; no new patient data.

Responsible use

This site demonstrates evaluation methodology. The models name a joint and nothing else. They must not be used for diagnosis, triage, implant identification or any patient-facing purpose. Please do not upload identifiable images.

Acknowledgements & data

Dataset creators are credited on the Datasets page; licences as reported by the hosts.

Project scopeThe models classify Hip, Knee or Shoulder. This project evaluates how joint-recognition models are tested; disease prediction and implant-brand identification are outside the implemented task.
