Critical synthesis

Three lessons.
One focused research gap.

01 / SHORTCUTS

High accuracy needs context.

Shortcut research motivates testing non-anatomical information. Our dimension and metadata controls make that question measurable on this benchmark.

02 / VALIDATION

Source separation matters.

External-validation work motivates holding out sources. Our public-source comparison does not substitute for clinical hospital-level validation.

03 / TASK DEFINITION

Related tasks are different.

Implant identity, abnormality detection and joint recognition have different labels and datasets. Cross-paper accuracy rankings would be misleading.

Our bounded gap

How much does pooled public-data evaluation overstate hip, knee and shoulder recognition when joint and source are confounded?

We address this question with six sources, anatomy-free baselines, multiple model families and source-controlled comparisons. We do not claim to have discovered shortcut learning or to be the first study of its kind.
