How to read the report.

  1. Expanded model scores Hip, Knee, Shoulder, Hand, Leg and Elbow independently.
  2. Pooled model — trained on all six sources. Its prediction appears at the top of the report.
  3. Three source-held-out models — each trained without one source (FracAtlas, hip implants, shoulder implants).
  4. Concordance — shows how many of the four checkpoints selected the same class as the pooled model.
  5. Grad-CAM — drag to compare the radiograph with a heatmap of regions that influenced the pooled model’s prediction.
Project scopeThe models classify Hip, Knee or Shoulder. This project evaluates how joint-recognition models are tested; disease prediction and implant-brand identification are outside the implemented task.
