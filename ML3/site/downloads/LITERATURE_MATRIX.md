## Critical synthesis and bounded gap

The literature serves three distinct purposes: shortcut/domain-shift studies explain why pooled scores need scrutiny; anatomical/implant applications establish related tasks and practical context; dataset and architecture papers identify the inputs and models. Their metrics and labels differ, so cross-paper accuracy rankings would be misleading.

The defensible research gap is an explicit, source-controlled comparison of hip/knee/shoulder recognition on this six-source public benchmark, with anatomy-free controls and saved paired predictions. It is **not** a claim to have discovered shortcut learning or demonstrated hospital failure. A targeted bibliography cannot prove no prior study exists.

The methodology follows from that gap: grouped splits address observed grouping; FracAtlas decouples source from target joint; metadata/dimension baselines test anatomy-free predictability; multiple backbones and seeds test consistency; paired cluster bootstrap and corrected hypotheses quantify uncertainty. One decoupled source, provisional labels and limited training constrain the inference.

To complete the highest literature criterion, obtain full texts, record dataset/task/model/evaluation split/results/limitations for at least 15 qualifying recent journal/conference papers, and verify venue/year metadata. Do not count preprints as journal papers without a confirmed version, duplicate preprint/journal versions as two papers, or abstract-level summaries as full-text critical reviews. The existing bibliography provides a starting set; this delivery does not certify that criterion as completed.
