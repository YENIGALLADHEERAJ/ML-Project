Six public sources, one with all three joints

Provisional knee labelsThe 94 FracAtlas knee images were labelled by visual inspection by an AI model, not by a clinician. A clinician review protocol and blinded review tool are prepared; results will be reported alongside the original-label analysis. Licences are as reported by the hosts and some need rights clarification (see dataset licence notes).

Not used

MURA, LERA and MRKR require data-use agreements and were not accessed. They are discussed as useful extensions (LERA as an independent replication), not as necessary for the central claim — see the necessity memo. No data were added to strengthen the results.

Project scopeThe models classify Hip, Knee or Shoulder. This project evaluates how joint-recognition models are tested; disease prediction and implant-brand identification are outside the implemented task.
